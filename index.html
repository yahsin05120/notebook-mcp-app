Gemini Notebook MCP (personal)

This is a private, single-user setup that lets the owner's own AI assistants (Claude, Codex, Hermes) manage the owner's own notebooks through the official Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) API.

It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.
