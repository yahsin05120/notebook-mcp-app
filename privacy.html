Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

  • This app is used only by its owner. It requests the Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/notebook solely to list, create and rename the owner's notebooks and add sources to them.
  • No data from any other person is collected. Notebook data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and the owner's own AI assistant accounts, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
